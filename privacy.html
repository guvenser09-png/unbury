Unbury — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

What we collect

  • A random device identifier — generated on your device, used only to keep your scores, streak and digger name together. It is not linked to your real identity.
  • Gameplay data — your daily scores, the moves of your counted runs (used to verify scores server-side), streaks, and the display name you choose.

What we don't collect

No email (unless you later opt in to account linking), no contacts, no location, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.

Where it lives

Game state is stored on your device. Verified scores, streaks and display names are stored on our server (Supabase, hosted in the EU) to power the world leaderboard.

Display names

The digger name you choose is shown publicly on leaderboards. Don't put personal information in it. Offensive names are filtered and may be reset.

Data removal

To remove your data, email guvenser09@gmail.com with your device ID (shown at the bottom of the in-game Settings screen).

Children

Unbury is suitable for general audiences and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
